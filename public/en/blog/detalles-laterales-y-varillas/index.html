Product presentation

The temple also tells the story of the frame

How to use the 360° view and side details to present a frame more clearly.

3 min← All articles

The front of a frame creates the first impression, but the side completes its personality. Temple details add visual talking points for both displays and consultative service.

Show the frame in motion

The 360° rotation reveals volume, proportions and design continuity. On the product page it can be alternated with the gallery without losing the selected variant.

Bring the finishes closer

Zoom helps review joints, tips and decorative accents. Use the thumbnails to understand the overall form first and then focus on individual details.

A complete presentation combines front, side, interior, tip and video views whenever the model source provides them.
